The Latin America funds.

Headline's Brazil Early Fund family: two generations with Redpoint eventures, the current fund with XP, and the global growth fund that follows our companies as they scale.

Now investing · 2022–
R$ 915 miHeadline Brazil III · Headline XP FIP

Headline's third Brazil fund, co-managed with XP Vista Asset Management. Raised in 2022 from more than 12,000 individual investors through XP, it was the first venture capital fund in Brazil to open early-stage investing to XP's clients at scale.

  • Seed to Series B, Brazil and Latin America
  • Minority stakes, typically up to 20%
  • Portfolio of around 25 companies over four years
  • Managed by Romero Rodrigues and the São Paulo team
Harvesting · 2012–2021
US$ 305 miRedpoint eventures I and II

Headline's first two Brazil funds, launched in 2012 as a joint venture between e.ventures (now Headline) and Redpoint Ventures. Fund I closed at US$ 130 million in 2012, the first dedicated early-stage fund of its size in the country.

  • Pismo, Olist, Rappi, Resultados Digitais, Creditas
  • Co-managed by Romero Rodrigues since 2015
  • Rebranded under Headline in 2021
Global · Series B and later
Global GrowthHeadline Global Growth IV · €776 mi

Headline's global growth fund invests in the best companies from every regional team as they scale. In Latin America it has backed Pismo, Creditas and Buk alongside the local fund.

  • Series B onwards, worldwide
  • Follows local-fund companies into growth rounds
  • Managed from San Francisco with the regional teams

Headline also runs early-stage funds in the United States, Europe and Asia. The full structure is described on headline.com/about.
